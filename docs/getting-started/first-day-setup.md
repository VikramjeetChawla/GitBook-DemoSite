# First-Day Setup

## First-Day Setup

### 1. Overview

Welcome to your first day at **\[Company Name]**!

This guide provides step-by-step instructions to help you set up your workstation, access your company accounts, configure essential applications, and prepare your work environment.

Please follow the steps in the order provided. If you encounter any issues during the setup process, contact the IT Support team for assistance.

**Objective:** Ensure that every new employee has a properly configured, secure, and functional work environment before beginning their assigned responsibilities.

### 2. Prerequisites

Before starting the setup process, ensure that you have received the following from the relevant team:

* Company-issued laptop or desktop computer.
* Company email address and account activation instructions.
* Initial login credentials or password setup instructions.
* Required access details for company systems and applications.
* Information about your department, reporting manager, and assigned team.
* IT Support contact details.

**Important:** Never share your password, verification codes, or other authentication credentials with anyone. If you have not received your login details, contact IT Support rather than attempting to create an account independently.

### 3. First-Day Setup Checklist

Complete the following steps to prepare your workstation.

#### Step 1: Set Up Your Workstation

* [ ] Collect your assigned laptop or desktop and any required accessories.
* [ ] Check that the device, charger, keyboard, mouse, and other peripherals are functioning correctly.
* [ ] Connect the device to a power source.
* [ ] Connect to the approved company network or Wi-Fi.
* [ ] Confirm that the operating system starts and functions normally.
* [ ] Report any hardware issues or missing equipment to IT Support.

#### Step 2: Sign In to Your Company Account

* [ ] Start your computer and sign in using your company-provided account.
* [ ] Follow the account activation instructions provided by IT.
* [ ] Change the temporary password if prompted.
* [ ] Set up multifactor authentication (MFA) if required by company policy.
* [ ] Verify that you can access your desktop and essential system settings.
* [ ] Confirm that your account is working correctly.

**Security note:** Use only the credentials assigned to you. Do not use another employee's account or share your own account with anyone.

#### Step 3: Configure Your Company Email

* [ ] Open the approved company email application or webmail portal.
* [ ] Sign in using your company email account.
* [ ] Complete any required authentication steps.
* [ ] Send a test email if instructed by IT Support.
* [ ] Confirm that you can receive emails.
* [ ] Review your email signature and configure it according to company guidelines, if applicable.

#### Step 4: Install and Verify Required Software

* [ ] Review the **Tools and Software** documentation.
* [ ] Identify the applications required for your role.
* [ ] Install approved applications using the company's authorized installation method.
* [ ] Sign in to the applications using your assigned company account.
* [ ] Verify that the essential applications launch and function correctly.
* [ ] Request additional software or access through the approved IT process if needed.

**Important:** Do not install unauthorized software or download applications from untrusted sources.

#### Step 5: Verify Access to Company Resources

* [ ] Open the internal documentation portal.
* [ ] Confirm access to the resources required for your role.
* [ ] Verify access to approved file storage, shared folders, or collaboration platforms, where applicable.
* [ ] Check access to your team's project management or communication tools.
* [ ] Confirm access to role-specific systems that have already been authorized.
* [ ] Report any permission errors or missing access to IT Support.

Access is granted according to your role and business requirements. Some systems may require approval from your manager or the relevant system owner.

#### Step 6: Configure Security Settings

* [ ] Ensure that your device has the required security software and protection enabled.
* [ ] Confirm that operating system updates are installed or scheduled according to IT guidelines.
* [ ] Enable screen locking and use a secure sign-in method.
* [ ] Configure MFA for supported company accounts, where required.
* [ ] Review the company's password and acceptable-use policies.
* [ ] Familiarize yourself with the process for reporting suspicious emails or security incidents.

Never disable security controls, bypass company restrictions, or store company credentials in unauthorized locations.

#### Step 7: Verify Communication Tools

* [ ] Sign in to the company's approved messaging and meeting applications.
* [ ] Check that your profile displays the correct name and company information.
* [ ] Test your microphone, speakers, and camera if required.
* [ ] Confirm that you can contact your manager and team.
* [ ] Review any team communication guidelines provided during onboarding.

#### Step 8: Complete the Final Verification

Before considering your first-day setup complete, verify that:

* [ ] You can sign in to your assigned device.
* [ ] Your company email is working.
* [ ] Required software is installed and accessible.
* [ ] Necessary company resources are available.
* [ ] Required security settings are enabled.
* [ ] Communication tools are functioning.
* [ ] Any pending access requests or setup issues have been reported.

| Email not working             | Verify your account access and report the issue to IT Support.                      |

### 5. Security and Compliance

All employees are expected to follow company security policies while using company devices, accounts, applications, and data.

* Keep your login credentials confidential.
* Lock your screen whenever you leave your workstation unattended.
* Use only approved devices, software, networks, and storage locations.
* Do not share confidential company information with unauthorized individuals.
* Report suspicious emails, unusual account activity, lost devices, or potential security incidents promptly.
* Follow the organization's IT policies and data-handling requirements.

Refer to the **Company Policies** document for the complete requirements.

### 6. Completion

Once all required setup steps are complete, you should be ready to begin your assigned work.

If any task remains incomplete because of pending approvals, missing credentials, or technical issues, inform your manager and IT Support so the necessary actions can be taken.

For additional guidance, refer to the following documents:

* **Tools and Software** — For application information and installation instructions.
* **Company Policies** — For workplace rules, security standards, and acceptable-use requirements.
* **IT Support Documentation** — For technical assistance and troubleshooting procedures.
