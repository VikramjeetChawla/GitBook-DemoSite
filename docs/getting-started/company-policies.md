# Company Policies

## Company Policies

### 1. Overview

Welcome to **\[Company Name]**.

This document outlines the essential workplace, IT, and information security policies that all employees are expected to understand and follow. These policies help maintain a secure, professional, respectful, and productive working environment.

All employees must familiarize themselves with the policies relevant to their roles and responsibilities. Where a formal company policy, employment agreement, or applicable law establishes additional or different requirements, the official document and applicable requirements take precedence.

### 2. Purpose

The purpose of these policies is to:

* Establish clear expectations for employee conduct and professional behavior.
* Protect company devices, systems, accounts, and confidential information.
* Promote responsible use of company-provided resources.
* Reduce information security risks and unauthorized access.
* Support a respectful, safe, and inclusive workplace.
* Ensure employees understand how to report incidents, concerns, and policy violations.

### 3. Code of Conduct and Professional Behavior

All employees are expected to act professionally, responsibly, and respectfully in the workplace.

Employees must:

* Treat colleagues, customers, partners, and visitors with respect.
* Communicate professionally in emails, meetings, chats, and other work-related interactions.
* Perform assigned responsibilities honestly and diligently.
* Respect other employees' privacy, time, and professional boundaries.
* Avoid harassment, discrimination, bullying, intimidation, and other inappropriate conduct.
* Follow company procedures and lawful, work-related instructions.
* Report serious misconduct or workplace concerns through the appropriate reporting channels.

The company aims to maintain a workplace where employees can work safely, collaborate effectively, and raise concerns without fear of retaliation.

### 4. IT Asset and Device Usage Policy

Company-issued devices and equipment are provided for authorized business activities.

Employees must:

* Use company devices in accordance with company policies and their intended purpose.
* Keep laptops, desktops, mobile devices, and accessories secure.
* Lock their screens when leaving devices unattended.
* Avoid lending company equipment or allowing unauthorized individuals to use it.
* Keep devices physically protected, especially when working remotely or travelling.
* Report lost, stolen, damaged, or malfunctioning equipment promptly.
* Return company assets when requested or when employment ends, following company procedures.

Employees must not modify device security settings, disable required security software, or attempt to bypass IT restrictions without authorization.

### 5. Password and Account Security Policy

Every employee is responsible for protecting their company accounts and authentication credentials.

Employees must:

* Use individual company accounts assigned to them.
* Create passwords that meet the company's approved password requirements.
* Use multifactor authentication (MFA) wherever required.
* Never share passwords, verification codes, authentication tokens, or recovery codes with others.
* Never use another employee's credentials to access company systems.
* Use an approved password manager when required or provided by the company.
* Report suspected account compromise or unauthorized access immediately.

Employees must follow the company's approved account recovery process if they forget their password or lose access to an authentication method.

### 6. Email and Internet Usage Policy

Company email and internet access must be used responsibly and in accordance with business and security requirements.

Employees must:

* Use official company communication channels for business correspondence.
* Verify unexpected requests for sensitive information or urgent payments.
* Avoid clicking suspicious links or opening unexpected attachments.
* Never send confidential company information to unauthorized recipients.
* Avoid using personal email accounts to transfer company information unless expressly authorized.
* Access only websites and services permitted by company policy.
* Avoid activities that expose company systems to malware, phishing, or other security threats.
* Maintain professional language and conduct in all work-related communications.

Suspicious emails, unexpected login prompts, and potential phishing attempts must be reported through the designated security reporting process.

### 7. Data Protection and Confidentiality Policy

Employees may access confidential information as part of their job responsibilities. Such information must be handled securely and shared only with authorized individuals.

Confidential information may include:

* Customer and client information.
* Employee and personnel records.
* Business plans, financial information, and internal reports.
* Source code, technical documentation, and system configurations.
* Credentials, API keys, access tokens, and other security-sensitive information.
* Contracts, intellectual property, and proprietary business information.

Employees must:

* Access information only when required for their assigned responsibilities.
* Store and share files using approved company systems.
* Apply appropriate access permissions when sharing documents.
* Verify recipients before sending sensitive information.
* Avoid storing confidential information in unauthorized personal accounts, devices, or cloud services.
* Follow company retention, disposal, and data-handling procedures.
* Protect confidential information during and after employment, as required by applicable agreements and policies.

Suspected data loss, accidental disclosure, or unauthorized access must be reported promptly.

### 8. Software Installation and Acceptable Use Policy

Only authorized and appropriately licensed software may be used for company business.

Employees must:

* Install applications through approved company methods.
* Obtain required approval before installing software that needs elevated permissions or additional licensing.
* Keep software updated according to IT requirements.
* Use company-approved tools for handling business information.
* Follow software licensing and intellectual property requirements.
* Avoid pirated, cracked, or unauthorized applications.
* Never disable security controls to install or run software.

Employees must not use company systems for unlawful activities, unauthorized access, malicious activities, or any activity that compromises the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of company resources.

### 9. Network and Remote Access Policy

Access to company networks and internal systems must follow the organization's approved security procedures.

Employees must:

* Connect to company systems using authorized accounts and approved access methods.
* Use the company VPN when required for remote access.
* Follow security guidance when working from public or shared networks.
* Avoid connecting unauthorized devices to company networks.
* Protect their screens and devices when working in public spaces.
* Avoid discussing sensitive company information where unauthorized individuals may overhear.
* Report suspicious network activity or unexpected access notifications.

Employees must not attempt to bypass network restrictions, scan systems without authorization, or access resources beyond their approved permissions.

### 10. Physical Security and Clean Desk Policy

Employees must take reasonable steps to protect company equipment, documents, and information from unauthorized access.

Employees should:

* Lock their workstation whenever they leave it unattended.
* Secure printed documents containing confidential information.
* Avoid leaving access cards, keys, or devices unattended.
* Store sensitive documents in approved secure locations.
* Dispose of confidential printed materials using approved disposal methods.
* Prevent unauthorized individuals from entering restricted work areas.

Visitors and contractors must follow applicable access and escort procedures.

### 11. Attendance, Leave, and Workplace Expectations

Employees are expected to follow the organization's official attendance, working-hours, leave, and time-reporting procedures.

Employees should:

* Follow the working schedule communicated by their manager or HR.
* Record attendance and working hours accurately when required.
* Request leave through the designated approval process.
* Inform their manager about unexpected absences or delays according to company procedures.
* Attend required meetings, training sessions, and onboarding activities.
* Follow the company's remote-work, hybrid-work, and office-attendance requirements where applicable.

For specific entitlements, approval timelines, and procedures, refer to the official HR policies or contact the HR team.

### 12. Remote Work Policy

Employees approved to work remotely must maintain appropriate security, availability, and professionalism.

Employees must:

* Use authorized devices and approved remote-access methods.
* Maintain a suitable working environment that protects company information.
* Keep devices and documents secure when not in use.
* Follow applicable attendance, communication, and availability expectations.
* Avoid using shared or public devices to access company accounts.
* Report device loss, suspected compromise, or security incidents immediately.

Remote work arrangements remain subject to company approval and applicable workplace policies.

### 13. Artificial Intelligence and External Tools

Employees must use artificial intelligence (AI) tools and external online services in accordance with company policy.

Unless expressly approved, employees must not:

* Enter confidential company data, customer information, personal data, credentials, source code, or proprietary material into public AI tools.
* Upload internal documents to unauthorized websites or third-party applications.
* Rely on AI-generated information without appropriate review and verification.
* Use external tools in ways that violate privacy, security, intellectual property, or contractual obligations.

Only company-approved AI tools and permitted use cases should be used for work involving company information.

### 14. Security Incident and Issue Reporting

Employees must promptly report suspected or confirmed security incidents, including:

* Lost or stolen company devices.
* Suspicious emails, links, or attachments.
* Suspected password or account compromise.
* Unauthorized access to systems or files.
* Accidental sharing or disclosure of confidential information.
* Malware alerts or unusual device behavior.
* Unexpected MFA requests or suspicious login notifications.

**Reporting procedure:**

1. Stop the activity that may be causing or contributing to the issue, if safe to do so.
2. Avoid deleting evidence, investigating beyond your authorization, or attempting unapproved fixes.
3. Contact IT Support or the designated security team through the approved reporting channel.
4. Provide a clear description of the issue, the time it occurred, and any relevant error messages or observations.
5. Follow the instructions provided by the authorized support or security team.

Employees should report incidents promptly and honestly, even when the incident may have resulted from an accidental mistake.

### 15. Privacy and Monitoring

Company systems, devices, networks, and accounts may be subject to monitoring or auditing where permitted by applicable law and company policy.

Such monitoring may support security, compliance, troubleshooting, and protection of company resources.

Employees must follow applicable privacy and data-handling requirements and should consult the official privacy notice or HR documentation for details about the information collected, the purposes of processing, and applicable employee rights.

### 16. Policy Violations

Failure to comply with company policies may result in corrective or disciplinary action, in accordance with applicable law, employment agreements, and established company procedures.

Depending on the circumstances, the company may restrict access to systems, require remedial training, investigate reported incidents, or take other appropriate action.

Employees are encouraged to raise concerns promptly through the appropriate reporting channels.
